Approach

Reuse what works.Reduce what distracts.Make the interaction carry the clarity.

01

I start from product logic and mental models, not decoration. The interface should explain how the system behaves, especially in complex, ambiguous problems.

02

I extend and advocate the design system before inventing new components, and keep it consistent enough that people and AI can both build from it.

03

I work cross-functionally, close to PM, engineering, and stakeholders, explaining the concept behind a screen, not just the screen, so handoff feels like a shared product conversation.

04

I prototype with engineering in mind, often in code or Codex, so the team can pressure-test states, feasibility, and edge cases before build cost rises.

05

I treat B2B as consumer-grade underneath. A business buys the product, but one person still has to build an audience, configure automation, and pay, largely on their own. So I design the permissions, complexity, and scale a business needs with the speed, clarity, and ease people expect from consumer apps.
