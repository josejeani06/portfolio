Pain points

Must name the automation before adding any location—a setup gate.

Map assumes you’ll draw; no path for ZIP, CSV, or saved areas.

Dead clicks and weak feedback; past areas can’t be reused.

No include/exclude—refine by deleting addresses one by one.

Slow filtering; overlapping selections are hard to read.

Support becomes part of setup—31 map-related tickets logged.

Multiple entry points; make the first click useful.

Include and exclude controls.
